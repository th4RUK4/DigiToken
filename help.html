Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a token? ▼
You can get a token in two ways:
  • Online Booking: Log in to the dashboard, click "Book Token", select your service and time slot.
  • Walk-in: Visit our service center and our staff will issue a Walk-in (W-xx) token for you.
What happens if I'm late? ▼
If you miss your called number, please report to the service desk immediately. We might be able to slot you in as a "No Show" recovery, or you may need to take a new Walk-in token.
How do notifications work? ▼
We send updates via Email and WhatsApp (if enabled). You will receive alerts when:
  1. Your token is generated.
  2. There are 5 people ahead of you.
  3. It is your turn to be served.
Can I cancel my token? ▼
Yes, you can cancel your pending token from the "Recent Activity" section in your Dashboard. This helps us serve others faster!
